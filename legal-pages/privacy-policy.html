At Manufactt, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you share with us when you interact with our digital platforms, services, and creative solutions.

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information (e.g., name, email address, phone number) — provided contact requests, or newsletters.

  • Project information — details you share with us regarding your brand, business, or project requirements.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

Respond to your inquiries or project requests

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes to this policy

Our story

Samruddhi Industries began its journey in 2021 with a simple thought to build a strong presence in the textile industry by creating fabrics that businesses could rely on. What started as a vision has grown through manufacturing experience, long-standing customer relationships and a constant focus on doing things the right way.

Every stage of our journey has taught us something new, from understanding fabric requirements to strengthening our production capabilities and building relationships that go beyond individual orders. Today, we continue to grow with the same belief we started with: when you build with consistency and earn trust along the way, growth follows.

Technology That Drives Our Production

Our machinery is selected around one objective: producing consistent greige fabric at scale. With high capacity waterjet weaving and integrated production infrastructure, we bring key stages of manufacturing under one roof.

Waterjet Looms

What they do: Weave Polyester and synthetic yarns into greige fabric using high speed waterjet technology.

Key capabilities:

  • 200+ waterjet machines
  • High speed weft insertion
  • Multiple fabric constructions
  • Large scale continuous production

Warping Machines

What they do: Prepare and arrange yarns into an organised warp sheet before weaving.

Key capabilities:

  • Controlled yarn tension
  • Precise yarn alignment
  • Consistent warp preparation
  • Production ready warp beams

Yarn Preparation Systems

What they do: Prepare yarn for smooth movement through the warping and weaving stages.

Key capabilities:

  • Controlled yarn handling
  • Consistent preparation
  • Production focused processing
  • Support for continuous manufacturing

Integrated Manufacturing Infrastructure

What it enables: Our production setup brings warping and fabric manufacturing together within our facility.

Key capabilities:

  • 1 lakh sq. ft. manufacturing area
  • Up to 70,000 metres produced per day
  • Integrated production flow
  • Large scale greige fabric manufacturing
